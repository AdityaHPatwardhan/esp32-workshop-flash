# ESP32-S3 Workshop

1. Plug the board in with a **data** USB cable (Chrome or Edge only).
2. Click **Connect**, pick the board's serial port, then **Flash**.
3. When flashing finishes, the console shows the boot log. Press **RESET** to see it again.
4. Type `team <your team name>` in the command box and press Enter,
   e.g. `team Rockets`. Your team appears on the projector within a second
   (do it again after flashing the next app: flashing wipes it).
   Nothing happens when you type? Tell the instructor (pick the *-usbconsole* app from the list and flash again).

Stuck? Hold **BOOT**, tap **RESET**, release **BOOT**, then click Connect again.
